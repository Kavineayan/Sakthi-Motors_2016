PRIVACY POLICY

How Sakthi Motors collects, uses, and safeguards customer information.

1. Information We Collect

Sakthi Motors ("we", "us", or "our"), an authorized Bajaj motorcycle dealership located in Chinnamanur, Theni district, Tamil Nadu, collects information that you provide directly when contacting our dealership or submitting our contact form. This information may include your full name, telephone number, email address, motorcycle preferences, and any specific inquiries or messages you provide.

2. How We Use Customer Information

We utilize collected customer information solely for legitimate dealership business operations, including:

  • Responding directly to vehicle inquiries, product availability, and service requests.
  • Providing technical specifications, documentation assistance, and showroom appointment guidance.
  • Assisting customers with authorized manufacturer warranty, registration, and insurance processes.
  • Improving our dealership customer service and communications in Theni district.

3. Data Protection & Security

Sakthi Motors is committed to ensuring customer personal details remain protected. We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards against unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. We do not sell, rent, or trade customer contact details to third-party marketing entities.

4. Authorized Service Partners

Customer information may only be shared with authorized automotive partners (such as Bajaj Auto Ltd., vehicle registration authorities, or approved financial/insurance providers) as necessary to complete your motorcycle purchase or servicing requirements.
