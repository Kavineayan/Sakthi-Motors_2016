ABOUT SAKTHI MOTORS

DEDICATED TO RIDERS IN THENI

Established in 2016 with 10+ years of experience, Sakthi Motors has built a strong relationship with riders and families across Theni district. Over the years, we have created memorable customer experiences while achieving important milestones through our commitment to sales excellence, quality service and customer satisfaction.

Established with an unyielding commitment to customer satisfaction, SAKTHI MOTORS stands as a premier authorized Bajaj motorcycle dealership serving Chinnamanur and Theni district, Tamil Nadu. Guided by our motto "Your Journey, Our Commitment", we connect riders with world-class engineering, dependable performance and motorcycles designed for different lifestyles.
